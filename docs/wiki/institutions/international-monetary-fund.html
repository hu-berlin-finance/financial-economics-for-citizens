International Monetary Fund

Modified

September 23, 2026

Role in the Rating Process

The International Monetary Fund supplies core macroeconomic and fiscal data for the minimal rating process. Its World Economic Outlook is the primary data source for Growth (C1), Inflation Differential (C2), Fiscal Capacity (C3), and Refinancing Need (R1) parameters. The Fiscal Monitor provides additional coverage of primary balances (C3). Article IV reports offer a cross-check for gross financing needs (R1). Additionally, loans issued under IMF programs count as off-market financing (R1-A*), reducing the market-exposed portion of sovereign debt.
