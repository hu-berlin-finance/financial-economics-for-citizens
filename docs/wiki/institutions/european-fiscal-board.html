European Fiscal Board

Modified

September 23, 2026

Role in the Rating Process

The European Fiscal Board is an independent advisory body. It evaluates how the European Union fiscal framework is implemented. This evaluation feeds into the assessment of backstop eligibility under task R6-A. It also informs the assessment of fiscal capacity under task C3. The Board provides independent checks on fiscal policy compliance and sustainability. These inputs help rating teams judge whether fiscal institutions are credible and effective.

Evidence by Country

Euro Area

  • The European Fiscal Board is invited as an observer to the Debt Sustainability Analysis working group.
