Economic Survey

Modified

September 23, 2026

Overview

The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) publishes this document type approximately every two years for each member country and selected other states. It reviews macroeconomic developments, fiscal policy, and structural reforms. This card currently draws on twenty documents covering Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, and Spain.

Use in the Rating Process

The document type feeds the minimal rating process and discretionary adjustments process for the following tasks:

  • Growth (C1*): It provides growth forecasts and assessments of potential growth.
  • Inflation Differential (C2*): It supplies inflation projections and analysis of price competitiveness.
  • Fiscal Capacity (C3*): It offers fiscal projections and assessments of debt sustainability.
  • Outlook Factors (R7*): It provides context on structural challenges, demographic pressures, and political risks.
