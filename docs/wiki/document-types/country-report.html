Country Report

Modified

September 23, 2026

Overview

The European Commission publishes Country Reports every spring as part of the European Semester for each euro-area member state. These documents analyze economic, social, and fiscal developments, as well as the implementation of earlier recommendations. The current card draws on twenty-one documents covering Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, and Croatia, all published in June 2026.

Use in the Rating Process

The Country Report feeds the discretionary adjustments process (DAP) by providing detailed qualitative and quantitative assessments for key parameters. It informs the assessment of Growth and competitiveness (C1), the Inflation Differential (C2), the Fiscal Capacity and medium-term fiscal plan (C3), Refinancing Need and debt sustainability (R1), the Backstop Eligibility context (R6), the banking sector (C5), and Outlook Factors such as geopolitical risks and structural vulnerabilities (R7*).
