Backstop Eligibility

Modified

September 23, 2026

In the MRP

A backstop is a commitment by an institution whose resources the market cannot exhaust, in practice the European Central Bank or European Stability Mechanism, to buy or guarantee the debt under stated conditions; it removes the crisis equilibrium without any purchases. The state follows bright lines: clean compliance with the EU fiscal framework and a low-risk European Commission debt-sustainability classification mean eligible; an open excessive deficit procedure with effective action established means watch; non-compliance means ineligible. If eligible, the exposure probability accounts for the backstop. Upgrades out of distress through a backstop or program event require the eligible state.

In the DAP

R6-A bright lines versus real eligibility (red flags: formal compliance with doubtful political eligibility, such as rule-of-law disputes or open conflict with EU institutions; formal ineligibility with credible political will to support). The bright lines are never overridden; the only response is monitoring, and a fragile eligible state is outlook material.
