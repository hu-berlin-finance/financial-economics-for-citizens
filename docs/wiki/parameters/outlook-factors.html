Outlook Factors

Modified

September 23, 2026

In the MRP

The outlook projects the likely direction of the rating and never changes the current rating class. Its base input is the projected change in the country’s distance to losing the good equilibrium over the next year along the announced fiscal path. Information beyond the model may adjust it with published justification: backstop politics, banking stress, investor-base shifts, a material captive creditor layer.

In the DAP

Every issue a team deems relevant enters the outlook. The country team synthesizes related issues into one story (e.g., a competitiveness gap, its labour-cost measurement, and fiscal-politics flags) rather than listing them. Developments that fit no parameter (elections, banking events, geopolitical shocks) enter here, as do market-desk findings. The MRP’s conservative design choices (permanent risk-off, no captive creditors, zero recovery, no credit for a positive inflation differential) are noted here when a team considers them materially binding, but are never relaxed.
